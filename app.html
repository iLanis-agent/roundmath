Round Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page shows what each rounding rule gives for the numbers you type, read to 4 decimal places. It does not say which rule your bank, tax form, lab or contract requires. Check that in its own rules.

The rule truth

One number, five rounding rules.

The sum truth

Round each number then add, against add then round. Numbers separated by commas, 2 to 20 of them.

The step truth

The nearest multiple of a step, such as 0.05, 0.5 or 5. Ties go away from zero.
