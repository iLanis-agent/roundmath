Round Math

Round the parts, then add. You may not get the rounded whole.

Boundary: arithmetic only Round Math shows what each rounding rule gives for the numbers you type, read to 4 decimal places. It does not say which rule your bank, tax form, lab or contract requires. Check that in its own rules.

The rule truth

2.5 to 0 places: half away from zero 3, half to even 2, toward zero 2, down 2, up 3.

The sum truth

Rounding each of 3 numbers to 0 places and adding gives 3; adding first and rounding gives 4. They differ by 1.

The step truth

Nearest 0.5 to 7.3: 7.5 (up 0.2).

Run your numbers
Round Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
